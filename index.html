Marine Life

Big Game Fish You Can Catch or See in Mirissa

Yellowfin Tuna

  • One of the fastest and strongest swimmers in the ocean.
  • Known for putting up an incredible fight on the line.
  • Plentiful in the deep waters off the southern coast of Sri Lanka.

Highly prized for both sport fishing and its delicious meat!

Blue Marlin

  • The ultimate trophy fish for any deep sea angler.
  • Spectacular acrobatic jumps when hooked.
  • Can weigh hundreds of kilos and swim at incredible speeds.

A thrilling catch that requires strength, teamwork, and a good captain.

Giant Trevally (GT)

  • A highly aggressive predator fish known for striking hard.
  • Usually found near reefs and drop-offs.
  • Requires heavy tackle to reel in.

An unforgiving fighter that tests the limits of your fishing gear.

Mahi Mahi (Dorado)

  • Famous for their vibrant neon green, yellow, and blue colors.
  • High-jumping aerobatics once hooked.
  • Often found swimming near floating debris or seaweed lines.

A beautiful, fast-growing fish that is both fun to catch and great to eat.

Wahoo

  • A torpedo-shaped fish renowned for its blistering first run.
  • Razor-sharp teeth and zebra-like stripes.
  • Typically caught high-speed trolling.

Get ready to hear the reel scream when a fast-moving Wahoo strikes!

Little Tuna

  • One of the most common and exciting catches in the Indian Ocean.
  • Known for their speed, strength, and beautiful dark stripes.
  • Frequently found in large schools near the Mirissa coastline.

A fantastic catch for both beginners and experienced anglers looking for a thrill!

We're here to help you plan your perfect deep sea fishing adventure in Mirissa! Whether you're a beginner or an experienced angler, our team is happy to assist with bookings, sea conditions, and tour details.

Do I need prior deep sea fishing experience?

Not at all! Our tours are perfect for beginners. You'll receive a full safety briefing and basic fishing techniques before your adventure begins. Our experienced crew will be with you at all times.

What should I bring for the tour?

We recommend bringing sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat, swimwear, and a towel. We'll provide all deep sea fishing gear, life jackets, and waterproof bags for your belongings.

Are life jackets provided?

Yes, life jackets are provided for every guest, and wearing them during the entire tour is mandatory for safety. We have sizes suitable for both adults and children.

What happens if the weather is bad?

Your safety comes first. If sea conditions are unsafe due to rain or strong winds, we'll reschedule your tour for another convenient time or offer a full refund.
